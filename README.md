# 📊 Vedakarna Retail Sales & Profit Intelligence Dashboard

---

## 1. Project Title / Headline
**Vedakarna Enterprise Sales & Profitability Analytics Dashboard**  
An interactive corporate intelligence dashboard engineered in **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate multi-year commercial performance (2021–2024), product category margin efficiency, regional geographic distribution, and client profitability profiles across the United States.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose
The **Vedakarna Sales & Profit Dashboard** is an operational and financial decision-support tool built to aggregate multi-year commercial transaction data into executive-level KPIs. Tracking over **$1.92M** in gross revenue and **$247K+** in net margin, the dashboard pinpoints revenue drivers across merchandise tiers, maps state-level retail footprint, evaluates top margin-generating client accounts, and analyzes annual category profit growth.

This tool is designed for category heads, retail merchandisers, and supply chain analysts to optimize margin contributions, manage inventory stockouts, and identify under-penetrated sales territories.

---

## 3. Tech Stack & Architecture
* 📊 **Microsoft Excel (Advanced Data Modeling):** Main dashboard interface using dynamic Pivot Tables, interconnected Pivot Charts, custom UI cards, and structured worksheets.
* 🔄 **Power Query (M Engine):** Processed the end-to-end data pipeline including dataset cleaning, data type validation, date hierarchy parsing, and schema normalization.
* 📐 **Business Logic & Formulas:** Integrated aggregate financial calculations, profit margin derivations, multi-condition logic, and reference lookups.
* 🎛️ **Multi-Tier Interactive Slicers:** Deployed synchronized global slicers (**Category**, **Year: 2021–2024**, and **Month: Jan–Dec**) with multi-pivot connectivity for seamless real-time cross-filtering.

---

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* 💰 **Total Sales:** **$19,28,888 ($1.93M)** gross merchandise value
* 📈 **Total Profit:** **$2,47,961 ($248K)** bottom-line earnings
* 🎯 **Net Margin Conversion:** **~12.85%** operational margin efficiency
* 👥 **Customer Base Distribution:** Multi-cohort tracking across fiscal cycles (spanning 500+ to 630+ active buyers per year)

---

## 5. Dashboard Modules & Core Visualizations

### 1. Merchandise Hierarchy & Profit Breakdown
* **Profit by Year & Category:** Clustered column tracking analyzing yearly profitability trends across **Technology**, **Office Supplies**, and **Furniture** from 2021 through 2024.
* **Sub-Category Sales Funnel:** Ranked revenue breakdown identifying volume drivers—led by **Phones ($2,79,464)** and **Chairs ($2,77,059)**—down through medium-tier supplies to low-ticket items (Labels, Fasteners).

### 2. Seasonality & Pacing
* **Sales by Month:** Visual seasonality analysis mapping revenue peaks during Q4 (**November at $234K**, **December at $241K**) and early post-holiday cycles (**February low of $60K**).

### 3. Customer & Geographical Intelligence
* **Top 5 Customers by Profit:** Bar chart detailing maximum value contributors (Tamara Chand: $8,981, Raymond Buch: $6,939, Sanjit Chand: $5,757, Adrian Barton: $5,203, Martinez: $3,883).
* **Sales by State (US Geospatial Map):** Filled map rendering market penetration and geographic revenue volume across US territories.
* **Customer Distribution Cohort:** Donut visual illustrating customer participation across 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024.

---

## 6. Business Impact & Strategic Recommendations
1. **Focus on High-Margin Categories:** Technology consistently drives top-tier absolute profits; low-margin sub-categories (Fasteners, Envelopes) should be bundled to raise average cart value.
2. **Q4 Demand Buffering:** Major sales concentration in November and December requires proactive inventory allocation and logistics scaling by late Q3 to capture maximum holiday turnover.
3. **Enterprise Key Account Care:** Top individual accounts generate a significant share of cumulative margin, justifying focused retention strategies and bespoke volume-tier discounting.
